LEVEL 1: DIRECT CALCULATION

Mean, median and mode

Waiting times are 4, 5, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 26 minutes. Calculate the mode, median and mean. Which measure best describes a typical wait?

Identify the statistical concept, state the relevant formula or rule, calculate carefully, and finish with an interpretation in context.

Detailed solution

Method. Order the data. The median is the average of positions 4 and 5 because n=8n=8; the mean uses all observations. Result. Mode =5=5; median =(6+7)/2=6.5=(6+7)/2=6.5; mean =70/8=8.75=70/8=8.75 minutes. Interpretation. The isolated 26-minute wait pulls the mean upward. The median is the most robust description of a typical wait, while the mean remains relevant for total capacity planning.

LEVEL 2: GUIDED CALCULATION

Weighted decisions

A course grade combines quiz 20%, project 30% and final examination 50%. A student scores 14, 16 and 11 out of 20.

  1. Calculate the weighted mean.

  2. Compare it with the unweighted mean.

  3. Explain why the two differ.

Identify the statistical concept, state the relevant formula or rule, calculate carefully, and finish with an interpretation in context.

Detailed solution

Method. Multiply each score by its weight and verify that weights sum to one. Result. x‾w=.20(14)+.30(16)+.50(11)=13.10\bar x_w=.20(14)+.30(16)+.50(11)=13.10. The unweighted mean is (14+16+11)/3=13.67(14+16+11)/3=13.67. Interpretation. The final examination receives the largest weight and is also the lowest score, so the weighted result is lower.

LEVEL 3: METHOD SELECTION

Geometric and harmonic means

  1. Revenue changes by +20%+20\%, −10%-10\% and +15%+15\%. Find the equivalent average annual growth rate.

  2. A driver covers two equal distances at 60 and 90 km/h. Find the average speed.

Identify the statistical concept, state the relevant formula or rule, calculate carefully, and finish with an interpretation in context.

Detailed solution

Method. Compound growth requires the geometric mean of growth factors; equal-distance speed requires the harmonic mean. Result. Growth factor G=(1.20×.90×1.15)1/3=1.0754G=(1.20\times.90\times1.15)^{1/3}=1.0754, so average growth is about 7.54% per year. Average speed H=2/(1/60+1/90)=72H=2/(1/60+1/90)=72 km/h. Interpretation. The arithmetic means, 8.33% and 75 km/h, ignore compounding and unequal travel times respectively.

LEVEL 4: SYNTHESIS

Choosing the centre

For each case choose a principal measure and justify it: employee salaries with a few executives; most common product size; average portfolio multiplier over four years; average price paid when quantities differ; satisfaction categories from very dissatisfied to very satisfied.

Identify the statistical concept, state the relevant formula or rule, calculate carefully, and finish with an interpretation in context.

Detailed solution

Result. Salaries: median, due to right skew and outliers. Product size: mode, because the most frequent category is required. Portfolio multipliers: geometric mean, because returns compound. Average price paid: quantity-weighted arithmetic mean, ∑piqi/∑qi\sum p_iq_i/\sum q_i. Satisfaction: median or mode plus the full distribution, because the scale is ordinal. Interpretation. The correct centre is determined by the question, scale and data-generating mechanism, not by habit.
