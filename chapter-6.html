LEVEL 1: DIRECT CALCULATION

Elementary index and rebasing

A service price rises from EUR 40 in 2023 to EUR 46 in 2025.

  1. Calculate the 2025 index with 2023=100 and the percentage change.

  2. An old series is 90, 100, 108 for 2022–2024 with 2023=100. Rebase it to 2022=100.

Identify the statistical concept, state the relevant formula or rule, calculate carefully, and finish with an interpretation in context.

Detailed solution

Result. I=46/40×100=115I=46/40\times100=115, so the price rose by 15%. For rebasing, divide by 90 and multiply by 100: 100.00, 111.11, 120.00. Interpretation. Rebasing changes the reference, not the underlying relative movements.

Identify the statistical concept, state the relevant formula or rule, calculate carefully, and finish with an interpretation in context.

Detailed solution

Method. Compute ∑p0q0=400\sum p_0q_0=400, ∑p1q1=525\sum p_1q_1=525, ∑p1q0=490\sum p_1q_0=490 and ∑p0q1=430\sum p_0q_1=430. Result. Detailed solution

Result. IPF=122.50×122.09=122.30I_P^F=\sqrt{122.50\times122.09}=122.30. LEVEL 3: APPLICATION

Fixed-base, chain and deflation

An index is 100 in year 0 and 110 in year 1. The year-2 link index relative to year 1 is 106. Nominal year-2 revenue is EUR 636,000.

  1. Construct the chain index for year 2.

  2. Deflate revenue into year-0 euros.

Identify the statistical concept, state the relevant formula or rule, calculate carefully, and finish with an interpretation in context.

Detailed solution

Result. Chain index =110×106/100=116.6=110\times106/100=116.6. Real revenue =636,000/(116.6/100)=545,455=636{,}000/(116.6/100)=545{,}455 euros approximately. Interpretation. Nominal revenue must be divided by the price-index factor, not merely reduced by 16.6%.

LEVEL 4: SYNTHESIS

Supplier dashboard decision

A buyer reports: value index 128, Fisher price index 120 and Fisher quantity index 106.67. Explain the three numbers, verify consistency, and give two cautions before attributing the price rise to supplier market power.

Identify the statistical concept, state the relevant formula or rule, calculate carefully, and finish with an interpretation in context.

Detailed solution

Result. Expenditure is 28% above base, prices about 20% above base and quantities about 6.67% above base. Consistency holds because 120×106.67/100≈128120\times106.67/100\approx128. Interpretation. The index is descriptive. Before claiming market power, check product-quality and mix changes, contract terms, input costs, exchange rates, basket coverage and whether weights remain representative.
